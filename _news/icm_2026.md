Matthew Ballard participated in the AI for Mathematics panel at the
[2026 International Congress of Mathematicians](https://plover.com/~mjd/misc/icm2026_schedule.html)
in Philadelphia. Moderated by Emily Riehl, the panel also included Bogdan
Georgiev, Javier Gómez-Serrano, Terence Tao, and Geordie Williamson.

The discussion focused on the impact of AI on mathematical research practice,
including theorem proving, writing and refereeing, the development of junior
researchers, and how the mathematical community can use new technology while
advancing human understanding of mathematics.
