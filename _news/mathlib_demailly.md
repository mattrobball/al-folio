[Mathlib](https://leanprover-community.github.io), the collaborative library
of formalized mathematics in Lean for which Matthew Ballard serves as a
maintainer, was awarded the
[2026 Jean-Pierre Demailly Prize](https://epiga.episciences.org/page/session-2026)
for Open Science in Mathematics. The international biennial prize recognizes
projects contributing to open science through scientific publishing,
mathematical collaboration, or free mathematical software.

The jury highlighted Mathlib's broad significance for the future of
mathematics, its central role in formalization and verification, and its
open and collaborative model for creating, checking, preserving, and reusing
mathematical knowledge at scale.
