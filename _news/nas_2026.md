Matthew Ballard participated in the National Academies of Sciences,
Engineering, and Medicine meeting *Organizing Mathematical Knowledge in the
Age of AI and Formalization* in Washington, D.C. The two-day meeting examined
how formalization and AI are changing the creation, organization,
verification, and use of mathematical knowledge.

Ballard served on the opening Panel on the Current Landscape and Challenges
in Formalization and AI, moderated by Melanie Matchett Wood, together with
Javier Gómez-Serrano, Jesse Han, Jaume de Dios Pont, and Jason Reed. The
[event page](https://www.nationalacademies.org/units/DEPS-BMSA-22-P-451/event/46979)
has more information.
