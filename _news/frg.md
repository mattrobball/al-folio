As part of an NSF Focused Research Group (FRG)
with [Christine Berkesch](https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~cberkesc/),
[Michael K. Brown](https://webhome.auburn.edu/~mkb0096/),
[Daniel Erman](https://math.hawaii.edu/~erman/),
[David Favero](https://favero.math.umn.edu/),
[Sheel Ganatra](https://sheelganatra.com/), and
[Andrew Hanlon](https://sites.google.com/view/ahanlon-math/home), Matthew Ballard
has been awarded a grant for the project *“Multigraded Commutative
Algebra, Toric Varieties, and Homological Mirror Symmetry.”* This collaboration
will unite expertise in commutative algebra, algebraic geometry, and symplectic
geometry to develop new tools, solve longstanding problems, and build
connections between these fields. The FRG will also provide training and
mentoring opportunities through workshops, seminars, and open computational
resources.
